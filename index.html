Federal Tax Withholding (W-4)

Federal brackets are IRS Pub 15-T for 2026 — re-verify each January when the IRS republishes updated thresholds.

Paycheck Deductions

401(k) Traditional and the items above reduce federal taxable wages, but NOT Social Security/Medicare wages (a traditional 401(k) deferral is income-tax-exempt only, not payroll-tax-exempt). Roth 401(k) and "Other Post-Tax" reduce net pay only.

Additional Deductions

Pre-tax here is treated like Medical/Dental/HSA/FSA above — exempt from federal AND Social Security/Medicare wages.

Additional Income Items

Both types add to taxable gross pay (federal, Social Security/Medicare, and 401(k)-eligible wages) every paycheck. "Addition to Gross" is real cash you actually receive (e.g. an LTD gross-up). "In/Out" is imputed income — it's taxed as if you received it, but the same amount is then subtracted back out of your net pay, since you never actually get the cash (e.g. employer-paid group term life above $50k coverage).
